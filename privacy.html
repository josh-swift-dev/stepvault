StepVault Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

StepVault is designed with privacy in mind. The app provides an easy way to view and understand activity information already recorded through Apple Health on your device.

Health Data

With your permission, StepVault may read activity information from Apple Health using Apple's HealthKit framework. This may include:

  • Step count
  • Walking and running distance
  • Active energy burned
  • Exercise minutes
  • Flights climbed

This information is used to display your activity, historical statistics, goals, trends, personal records, and achievements within StepVault.

HealthKit Privacy

StepVault only accesses HealthKit information that you explicitly choose to share with the app.

StepVault does not sell, rent, or use HealthKit information for advertising or marketing purposes.

You can control or revoke StepVault's access to health information through Apple's Health settings at any time.

Data Collection

StepVault does not require you to create an account.

The current version of StepVault does not include an advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or StepVault-operated cloud database for storing your health information.

Data Sharing

StepVault does not sell your personal information or health information. Your HealthKit information is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Location

StepVault does not require location tracking to provide its primary step-history and activity features.

Children's Privacy

StepVault is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's health information access is controlled through Apple's HealthKit and device permission systems.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as StepVault evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
